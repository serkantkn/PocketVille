What's inside

You plan the city. The people build it.

Zone it, they build it

Paint residential, commercial and industrial zones next to your roads. If there's demand, settlers move in and build. Watch the three demand bars and give the city what it's asking for.

Four eras

Start with timber houses and a well, then grow through the industrial age, the modern city and the metropolis. Neighbourhoods rebuild in the new style once electricity reaches them, so old quarters stand next to new ones.

Utilities and services

Water and power flow along your roads. Police, fire, clinics, schools, parks and waste each cover an area, and each has a budget. Buildings left without them empty out, and land value tells you where it hurts.

Traffic that's real

Every commute and shopping trip takes a real route, and roads jam when they fill up. Upgrade to avenues and boulevards, lay one-way streets and tree-lined roads, and run trams, buses and the metro.

A city with a life of its own

Windows light up at dusk, cars and people fill the streets, factories smoke. Fires break out where the fire service can't reach. Festivals, tourists and trade missions turn up, and your advisors tell you what's wrong.

It keeps going while you're away

A game day is about a real minute, and the city keeps living for up to 12 hours while the app is closed. Sign in if you like and your city follows you between your phones.

Era by era

From a market square to a skyline

Each era unlocks new zones, roads and buildings. The heart of the city grows with you.

  1. Village

    Where you start

    Paths, wells, a market, a chapel and a watch post. Houses with gardens and little workshops.

  2. Industrial

    800 people

    Power plants, water pumps, factories with truck yards, schools, the train station and trams.

  3. Modern

    4,000 people and a university

    High density, hospitals, police and fire stations, buses, boulevards, highways and an airport.

  4. Metropolis

    20,000 people and a landmark tower

    Glass towers, the metro, wind and solar power, recycling, and landmarks that bring in tourists.

Cloud save is optional: sign in with Google on Android, or with Apple or Google on iPhone, and your city moves between your devices. Everything works without an account. The Mac version keeps its city on the Mac for now.

Trouble installing?

Android: Download the APK and open it. Your phone may ask you to allow installs from this source; allow it and finish the install.

Mac: Open the DMG and drag PocketVille into Applications. The app isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS stops it the first time: open System Settings → Privacy & Security and press “Open Anyway” at the bottom.

This is a beta, so you may run into bugs. Opening an issue on GitHub helps a lot.

Privacy

No ads, analytics or tracking. If you don't sign in, your city stays on your device.

If you sign in, your city save and the name of the device that saved it are stored in Google Firebase under your account. You can delete your account and cloud save at any time from Settings in the game.

At launch the game asks the internet for the time, so moving the clock forward doesn't pay off. This site uses no cookies; it only asks GitHub for the latest version.
